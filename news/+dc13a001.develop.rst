Convert the two pure data holders in ``zc.buildout.annotations`` to
dataclasses: ``HistoryItem`` becomes ``@dataclass(frozen=True)`` (it is
never mutated after construction) and ``SectionKey`` becomes
``@dataclass(eq=False)`` with a declared ``history`` field, keeping its
custom ``__init__(value, source)`` and identity equality. Both keep
their custom ``__repr__``, so ``buildout annotate`` output is
unchanged. [gt-builder]
