The pytest and coverage-pytest lanes no longer leak a ``PYTHONPATH=eggs/v5/*.egg`` glob into spawned children: the ``bin/test`` build now writes a flag-gated ``buildout-eggs.pth`` (generated from ``bin/py``'s sys.path by ``etc/write-buildout-eggs-pth.py``) into the venv's site-packages, and the lanes arm it with ``ZC_BUILDOUT_SUITE_EGGS=1``. Bare-interpreter xdist workers still find the checkout's ``src/`` and eggs, while the pytest conftest scrubs the flag from sample buildouts tests spawn, so children resolve the index instead of the repo's develop eggs.
