The legacy egg unpacker no longer uses ``setuptools.archive_util``:
``zc.buildout.install_backend`` now unpacks egg archives (zip, tar, or a
pre-built directory) with the stdlib only.  The replacement keeps the
setuptools semantics of skipping absolute or traversing archive entries
and of restoring unix permission bits on extracted files.  This removes
the ``setuptools`` import from the uv-mode install backend.
